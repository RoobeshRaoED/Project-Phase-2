Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Applies to all HLMS stakeholders — customers, loan officers, bank office, legal team, bidders, employees and admins.

Scope Data we handle Where data lives Role-based access Security Your choices Changes Contact

1. Scope

This policy explains how the Housing Loan Management System (HLMS) handles information for everyone who signs in — customers applying for a loan, loan officers, bank office staff, legal team members, bidders participating in auctions, employees, and administrators.

2. Data we handle

Depending on your role, HLMS may handle:

  • Account details — name, ID type/number, and role.
  • Loan application data — KYC documents, address proof, bank statements and property documents.
  • Property & legal records — valuation reports, tax receipts, legal verification notes and notices.
  • Case status — application stage, approval decisions, overdue/NPA flags, and auction or bid records.

3. Where data lives

This build of HLMS is a demonstration environment. All the information above is stored locally in your own browser and is not transmitted to, or stored on, any external server.

Because data stays on your device, it will not sync across browsers or devices, and clearing your browser storage will remove it. Treat this build as a working demo rather than a system of record.

4. Role-based access

Every sign-in is scoped to one role — Customer, Loan Officer, Bank Office, Legal Team, Bidder, Employee or Admin. Each role only sees the dashboards, cases and documents relevant to its work in the loan lifecycle.

5. Security

Access to each portal requires a valid role account and password. As with any demo system, do not use real personal, financial or identity documents when testing HLMS.

6. Your choices

You can review or remove the information stored in your browser at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this application, or by using the account tools available inside your portal.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HLMS evolves — for example, if a future version introduces server-side storage. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or how HLMS handles information can be directed to your system administrator.
