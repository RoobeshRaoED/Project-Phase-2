What HLMS handles

Everything a housing loan needs, in one system

Purpose-built modules cover the full loan lifecycle so no case falls through the cracks.

📄

Application & KYC

Structured intake for identity, address and bank-statement documents, with clear checklists for customers to follow.

🏠

Property & legal verification

Property valuation reports and tax receipts are reviewed alongside legal sign-off before a case can proceed.

✅

Approval workflow

Loan officers move applications through a defined review pipeline with full visibility into pending queues.

⚠️

Overdue & NPA tracking

Bank office teams monitor repayment health and flag accounts that need intervention before they escalate.

🔨

Auction & SARFAESI

Legal and bank office teams manage notices and auction scheduling; bidders view active listings and bid transparently.

🔎

Global search & alerts

Every stakeholder can search across their cases instantly and stays informed through in-app notifications.

How a case moves through HLMS

A single, ordered path — so every stakeholder always knows what stage a case is at, and whose turn it is to act.

1
Apply

Customer submits application & KYC documents

2
Verify

Legal team checks property & mortgage documents

3
Approve

Loan officer reviews and decides

4
Disburse

Bank office activates the account

5
Monitor

Overdue & NPA status is tracked

6
Resolve

Auction & SARFAESI, if required
