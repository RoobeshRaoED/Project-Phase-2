Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Please review how HLMS handles your information before registering.

Last updated: July 2026 · Demo build — all data is stored locally in your browser only.

1. Overview

This Housing Loan Management System (HLMS) is a demonstration application. It has no live backend server; all accounts, applications, and documents you create are stored in your browser's local storage and are never transmitted to Anthropic, a bank, or any third party.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details you enter at registration: name, mobile number, email, ID type/number.
  • Loan application data: employment, income, property, and uploaded document metadata.
  • Usage data such as application status, bids placed, and support requests, all kept locally.

3. How Information Is Used

Data you provide is used only to power the on-screen demo — eligibility calculations, workflow tracking, notifications, and the e-auction marketplace. Nothing is shared externally.

4. Data Storage & Security

All records live in your browser's localStorage under this site's origin. Clearing your browser data will permanently remove your accounts and applications. Because this is a demo, do not enter real personal, financial, or identity information.

5. Stakeholder Roles

Customer, Admin, Legal Team, Bank Office, and Bidder roles each see only the views and actions relevant to that role, as reflected in the sidebar navigation after login.

6. Cookies

HLMS does not use cookies or third-party trackers. Session state is kept via localStorage only.

7. Your Choices

You can deactivate or remove your demo account at any time from Profile Settings, or simply clear your browser's site data to erase everything.

8. Contact

For questions about this demo, use the in-app Support / Help section available to logged-in customers.
